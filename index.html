Privacy Policy — Number Merge Challenge

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Summary

Number Merge Challenge is a single-player puzzle game. Your game progress is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not collect personal information ourselves. The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect data as described below.

Information we store on your device

The app saves the following locally using your device's storage (WebView localStorage). It never leaves your device unless you back up your device:

  • level progress (levels unlocked and cleared),
  • best score and win streaks,
  • best tile/endless stats,
  • your sound/vibration preferences and whether you have seen the tutorial.

You can erase all of this at any time from Settings → Reset progress, or by clearing the app's data in Android settings. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Information collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate (IP-based) location,
  • device information (model, OS version, language) and ad interaction data.

This data is used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. It is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services

Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), the app requests consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before serving personalized ads. You can change your choice later from the privacy options form if one is offered, or by reinstalling the app.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with third parties other than Google (via AdMob) as described above.

Security

Game progress stays in local device storage. Because we do not operate servers, there is no account and no server-side copy of your progress to secure.

Your choices

  • Disable personalized ads: through your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads) or the in-app consent form where available.
  • Remove stored data: Settings → Reset progress, or Android app settings.
  • Opt out of ads entirely: not currently possible; ads fund the game.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
